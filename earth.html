About Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun. Around 71% of its surface is covered by water. Its atmosphere helps regulate temperature and protects life. Earth travels around the Sun once approximately every 365.25 days.

Earth's Key Features

Atmosphere: Mostly nitrogen and oxygen, with smaller amounts of other gases.
Surface: Oceans, continents, mountains, forests and deserts.
Temperature: The global average surface temperature is about 15°C, though it varies by location and measurement method.
Natural Satellite: The Moon affects ocean tides and is Earth's only permanent natural satellite.
